Why your business needs a website

Most customers look you up online before they get in touch. A good website makes sure they find you and like what they see.

  • Get found on Google

    When someone searches for “plumber in Herne Bay”, a website gives you a chance to show up. A Facebook page alone often won't.

  • Look like the real deal

    A clear, modern site tells people you're an established business they can trust, before they've even spoken to you.

  • Take enquiries around the clock

    Your site answers questions and collects enquiries in the evening, at weekends and while you're busy on a job.

  • Show off your work

    Photos, before-and-afters and reviews do the selling for you, so customers know what to expect.

  • Make it easy to get in touch

    Tap-to-call, WhatsApp and quote forms turn someone browsing on their phone into a real enquiry.

  • Have a home you own

    Social media rules change all the time. Your website and your domain name belong to you.

Common questions

How much does a website cost?

It depends on how many pages and features you need. I keep my prices affordable for small businesses, and you'll know the price before any work starts.

I already have a website. Can you improve it?

Yes. Send me the link and I'll tell you what's holding it back, whether that's speed, how it looks on a phone, or how easy it is to get in touch.

Do I need to be good with computers?

No. I handle the technical side, including the domain name and getting the site live. You just tell me about your business.

What do I need to give you?

Your logo if you have one, and photos of your work. Real photos work far better than stock images. I can help write the wording.

Will my site show up on Google?

Every site is set up with the basics Google looks for, like clear page titles, fast loading and your location. Nobody can honestly promise the top spot, but a well-built site gives you a real chance.
